# prospect — method rationale (loaded on demand only)

**Organic beats invented** — "the way to get startup ideas is not to try to think of startup ideas. It's to look for problems, preferably problems you have yourself" (pg, How to Get Startup Ideas). Own-friction mining is ranked first for this reason; the owner's own history (noCMS out of wiki-hosting dissatisfaction, unchained out of Django/FastAPI friction) matches the pattern.

**Evidence-artifact-only** — opportunities generated "off the top of our heads" import bias; limiting the opportunity space to real signals guarantees real unmet needs (Torres, opportunity solution trees). Hence the invariant: no brainstorms presented as findings.

**Why-now as a first-class field** — "recently possible" is a top discriminator in YC's idea evaluation (Friedman); a desire newly unblocked by a platform shift beats a merely unserved one. Its absence is a downgrade.

**Graveyard ≠ opening** — consumer-attractive ideas with warm feedback and a graveyard of near-clones are tarpits; predecessors weren't incompetent (Caldwell, YC). Competition validates the problem space (Friedman) — the check distinguishes "validated space with a real gap" from "the magical place doesn't exist".

**Grading and audit** — signals graded on two independent axes (source reliability × item credibility, Admiralty), traced to origin, copies discounted, protocol recorded so empty results are auditable (PRISMA-lite). Same evidence kernel as everywhere in forge.

**Anti-patterns excluded by invariant** — trend listicles and AI-generated idea lists (synthetic demand, no traceable artifact); "would-be-cool" framing without a sufferer; auto-advancing discoveries to build (prospect → judgment → validate is the pipeline; each arrow is a human decision).

# validate — method rationale (loaded on demand only)

Why the capability is shaped this way. Explanation quadrant; not procedure.

**Theory + pre-registered thresholds** — the strongest causal evidence in entrepreneurship research: RCTs totaling 759 firms show founders taught a scientific approach (explicit theory → ranked hypotheses → rigorous tests) terminate bad ideas ~2.7 weeks earlier (+9.8pp termination), pivot exactly once more often, and earn more (Camuffo et al., Management Science 2020; SMJ 2024 replication). The payoff of discipline is *faster kills and one focused pivot* — not more testing.

**Bounded testing** — no linear relation between number of validated hypotheses and success; over-testing produces false negatives and demoralization (Ladd, HBR 2016: "The Limits of the Lean Startup Method"). Hence: decision rules per test, and diminishing-returns flagging. Related critique: optimizing only what is easily measured yields incremental ideas (Felin et al., Long Range Planning 2020).

**Evidence-artifact-only opportunities** — opportunities brainstormed "off the top of our heads" import bias; limiting the opportunity space to real signals guarantees real unmet needs (Torres, opportunity solution trees, producttalk.org).

**Mom Test hygiene** — compliments, fluff, and hypotheticals are the three bad-data types; commitment (time, reputation, money) is the signal; "would you use this?" is worthless (Fitzpatrick, The Mom Test).

**Ladder** — Buffer's canonical sequence: two-page landing test, then a pricing page inserted *before* code was written; idea → paying customer in 7 weeks (buffer.com/resources/idea-to-paying-customers-in-7-weeks-how-we-did-it). Concierge/Wizard-of-Oz per Ries. Presale as strongest currency per Mom Test/Altman.

**YC filters** — organic vs made-up ("sitcom ideas" attract polite non-rejection: pg, How to Get Startup Ideas); tarpits — consumer ideas with warm feedback and a graveyard, where "the magical place doesn't exist" (Caldwell, YC); competition validates the problem space; idea-space base rates differ drastically (Friedman, YC); "large part of a small market" (Altman).

**Bottom-up sizing only** — top-down "1% of $X B" destroys credibility and predicts nothing; count reachable accounts × price × conversion via one named channel (TechCrunch TAM/SAM/SOM practice; Walling: one product, one channel).

**Synthetic-evidence ban** — synthetic users are sycophantic, fabricate exactly the past-behavior data that matters, and collapse variance (NN/g; MeasuringU review: 6 of 14 replications had too little variability to analyze); LLMs overestimate willingness-to-pay 2–3× on novel features (MSI/HBS Report 25-136). Sanctioned uses: desk research, interview-guide piloting, narrowing feature lists before human tests.
